### A tighter limit through a turn

Trying 8 orientations gives a different photo 8 chances to land close by luck,
and on the fixtures it does: the two pier shots (different photos of one place)
are 11 bits apart straight but 8 in their closest orientation. With the one
limit of 6 that still keeps them apart, but the room above the limit fell from
4 bits to 2. So a match through a turn has its own limit, 5, set the way 6 was:
in the middle of the measured gap.

| Pairs (closest orientation) | Bits |
|---|---|
| Turned/mirrored copy to the rest of the lake | **0–1** |
| Different photos | **8–14** |
| Straight, same photo (unchanged) | 0–2 |
| Straight, different photos (unchanged) | 10–21 |

## 2. PNG Compress: no lossless "≈" first (10-27 leftover)

Below Quality 100 a PNG is color-reduced, and what that saves depends on the
picture: a flat logo shrinks a lot, a photo hardly. The formula cannot see the
picture, so no estimate can account for it honestly. I took the async grammar's
route: while a color-reduced PNG is being measured, the readout is a skeleton
(Loading), and it falls back to the "≈" estimate only if the measurement fails,
so it never waits forever. Lossless PNG and the other formats keep their "≈".

| e2e `png-compress-estimate.spec.ts` (flat-art PNG, 1200 × 800) | Master | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Readouts with "≈" after the first step below the opening 75% | **"≈ 57 KB"** on every sample | **none** |
| Lossless size (measured) | 31 KB | 31 KB |
| Measured at Quality 60 | 15 KB | 15 KB |

So the old "≈" was nearly 4× the real number.

**Two test drafts were wrong first, and the log should say so:**

| Draft | Why it was wrong |
|---|---|
| 1. Record only CHANGES, starting at the first step | **Passed on master.** The lossless "≈" was already on screen and never changed while stepping, so a MutationObserver recorded nothing. Now it also samples the readout after every key press |
| 2. Record from the PNG click | **Failed on the fix too.** At the opening 75% nothing is pending, so the panel previews Apply Resize: the PNG's own (lossless) encoding, where "≈ lossless" is the right number. The case asked for starts at the first quality step |

Unit tests (`ResizeSettings.state.test.tsx`): skeleton until measured, red first
("≈ 88 KB"); fallback to "≈" when the measurement fails; lossless PNG and WebP
keep "≈".

**Parked (seen while testing):** on a format click the readout shows the
previous format's "≈" for a moment (WebP's "≈ 13 KB" under PNG), because the
estimate is effect-set state. In PARKING_LOT next to the fixed entry.
