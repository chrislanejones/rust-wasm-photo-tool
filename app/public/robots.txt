# Crawling is ALLOWED here and indexing is not, which sounds backwards and is
# the only combination that works. The "do not index this host" instruction is
# the <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> in index.html, and a crawler has to
# fetch the page to read it. Disallow it here instead and Google never sees the
# noindex — it just stops fetching, and the URL can still be listed from inbound
# links, as a result with no title and no description. So: let it in, and let the
# page turn it away.
#
# The pages worth ranking are all on https://imagehorse.app, which has its own
# robots.txt and the sitemap.
